DROPZONE

Drop onto a hostile planet. Do the job. Get out alive.
A first-person drop-trooper game with pixel-art worlds, running entirely in your browser.

▶ PLAY NOWWHAT IS THIS?

Desktop browser with mouse and keyboard · one ~8 MB file, nothing to install · progress saves in your browser

THE WAR FOR SECTOR DRACO

A war you fight one drop at a time

Walk your ship

Between missions you're aboard the SES Starfall: plot a course at the war table, dress your trooper in the armory, buy gear from the Quartermaster, upgrade the ship and watch its batteries shell the planet below.

Drop in a hellpod

Pick an operation, seal the pod and fall. Hold the objective, call stratagems with arrow codes, then hold the extraction pad until the Pelican comes.

Bugs and mercenaries

Hives of Scavengers, Spitters, Warriors and Chargers on some worlds; Skull Company mercenaries in others, who take cover, flank and radio for drop pods of reinforcements.

Hand-made worlds

A toxic swamp jungle with heavy rain and lightning, a desert of dunes, mesas, oases and giant bones, and more to come. Every operation is generated fresh.

A campaign that moves

Liberate worlds, defend them from counter-attacks, rise through ten ranks, earn medals and declassify new weapons and stratagems.

Twelve ship modules

Extra hellpods, coolant lines, Eagle bays, orbital scanners, sample satchels and more: every upgrade changes how your next drop plays.

FIRST DROP
  • Press Play, then Board your ship. The first time, a short tutorial walks you through a drop.
  • Click the game window to capture the mouse.
  • Walk to a Starfall pod in the launch bay and press E to pick an operation.
  • Your progress lives in this browser's local storage; clearing site data resets it.
